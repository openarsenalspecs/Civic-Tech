# Fundamenta

## Empowering Citizens Through Open Finance

## Overview

Fundamenta is an open-source **financial governance engine** designed for municipal, state, and federal applications. It combines complete government accounting, AI-assisted financial oversight, transparent public reporting, and rules-based budget governance into a modular platform.

Fundamenta creates a verifiable financial system where every budget item, transaction, vendor payment, and allocation decision can be tracked, explained, audited, and publicly understood.

The platform focuses on:

- Transparent public budgeting
- Complete financial accountability
- AI-assisted auditing and optimization
- Verifiable rules-based fund allocation
- Public visibility into government spending
- Efficient management of taxpayer resources

---

# Design Principles

## Transparency First
All approved financial activity should be traceable, explainable, and accessible through public reporting systems.

## Auditability
Every transaction, modification, approval, and AI recommendation maintains a complete audit trail.

## Modular Architecture
Fundamenta is designed with a modular architecture allowing governments to deploy only the functionality they need while extending capabilities through optional plug-in modules.

## Local Control
Governments maintain ownership and control over their financial data, workflows, and deployment environments.

## Explainable AI
AI recommendations must provide reasoning, supporting information, and confidence indicators.

## Rules-Based Governance
Financial rules, allocation policies, and approval processes are configurable, transparent, and verifiable.
