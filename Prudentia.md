# Prudentia

**Every Recommendation Has Consequences.**

Prudentia is an open source Enterprise Strategic Intelligence Platform that helps organizations avoid boom and bust cycles through explainable AI, human guided strategy development, long term consequence modeling, and comprehensive business resilience analysis. Rather than optimizing for short term profits alone, Prudentia evaluates every recommendation through the lens of mutual prosperity, ensuring business growth, customer success, and sustainable competitive advantage.

The platform is designed around a simple philosophy:

> Every recommendation creates consequences. Understand them before making the decision.

---

# Vision

Modern business intelligence systems optimize for growth.

Prudentia optimizes for survival, resilience, adaptability, and sustainable prosperity.

The platform continuously identifies hidden weaknesses, exposes blind spots, evaluates long term impacts, and helps organizations build businesses capable of thriving through changing markets, technological disruption, regulatory change, and economic uncertainty.

---

# Core Principles

- Human vision always comes before AI recommendations.
- The AI never assumes.
- Every recommendation is explainable.
- Every recommendation is stress tested.
- Every recommendation has measurable consequences.
- Customer prosperity should reinforce business prosperity.
- Long term resilience is prioritized over short term optimization.
- No blind spots.

---

# Human in the Loop

Prudentia never begins strategic analysis without first understanding the organization.

The platform conducts a structured interview to build a complete Business DNA model before generating recommendations.

If required information is missing, the AI asks additional questions until sufficient confidence is achieved.

# Dynamic Question Engine

Questions are generated dynamically based upon previous responses.

Examples include:

- What assumptions support your pricing strategy?
- How dependent are you on a single supplier?
- What happens if your primary market disappears?
- How does your product improve your customer's financial position?
- What measurable customer outcomes define success?

# Karmic Cycle Engine

The Karmic Cycle is the platform's primary strategic reasoning framework.

Every recommendation is evaluated through a complete consequence model.

The platform examines:

- Immediate effects
- Secondary effects
- Third order consequences
- Long term business impact
- Customer impact
- Employee impact
- Supplier impact
- Community impact
- Regulatory impact
