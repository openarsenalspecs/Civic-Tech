# RetailHub

### From Questions to Purchases.

RetailHub is a modular, AI-powered Commerce Operating System designed to help retailers, e-commerce businesses, and marketplaces connect customers with products through natural language interactions. RetailHub combines product discovery, customer intelligence, promotions, pricing, procurement, automation, manufacturer intelligence, review intelligence, and in-store navigation into a single extensible platform.

The platform is designed under a modular architecture, allowing organizations to enable only the functionality they need while maintaining a unified data and intelligence ecosystem.
