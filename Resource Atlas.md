# Resource Atlas

**A Global Tool for Local Prosperity.**

Resource Atlas is an open-source intelligence platform designed to help nations, states, provinces, municipalities, and communities identify resources, workforce gaps, infrastructure needs, strategic vulnerabilities, and opportunities for rapid revitalization.

The platform operates on a resilience-first model that assumes global trade may become less reliable over time. Rather than optimizing for exports, Resource Atlas focuses on helping regions strengthen local production, develop domestic capabilities, reduce critical dependencies, and build long-term prosperity from existing resources and knowledge.

Resource Atlas generates actionable **36-month revitalization roadmaps** for every sector of an economy, helping decision-makers understand current capabilities, identify shortages, evaluate alternatives, and implement practical solutions.
