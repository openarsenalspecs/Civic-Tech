# CharityLens

**Amplify what matters most.**

CharityLens is an AGPL-3.0+ open-source AI system for planning, validating, promoting, and optimizing charitable fundraising campaigns. It combines campaign strategy, trust and legitimacy, funding research, digital PR, media outreach, donation flow optimization, legal and compliance guidance, project tracking, and impact measurement into a unified campaign intelligence platform.

CharityLens is designed to help individuals, community organizations, nonprofits, and charitable initiatives turn an idea into a structured campaign with a realistic execution plan, credible supporting information, appropriate funding infrastructure, targeted public outreach, and measurable results.

The system is modular by design. Core modules provide the primary campaign intelligence and operational capabilities, while optional plugin modules extend CharityLens with integrations, external services, specialized data sources, analytics systems, and platform-specific functionality without making those services mandatory.

## Core Principles

CharityLens is built around the following principles:

- Transparency over unsupported claims
- Verification over assumption
- Human approval over autonomous outreach
- Publicly available information over private data harvesting
- Ethical promotion over spam
- Measurable impact over vague outcomes
- Modular architecture over vendor lock-in
- Open-source infrastructure over proprietary dependency
- Clear separation between AI recommendations and user decisions
- Compliance awareness throughout the campaign lifecycle

## Human Approval & Safety Controls

CharityLens is designed as an AI-assisted system rather than an autonomous fundraising agent.

The system should require user review and approval for consequential actions, including:

- Publishing campaign content
- Sending media outreach
- Contacting external organizations
- Publishing donation claims
- Publishing beneficiary claims
- Selecting fundraising platforms
- Making legal or compliance determinations
- Executing financial transactions
- Publishing impact claims

AI recommendations should remain distinguishable from verified facts and user-provided information.

## Ethical Outreach Requirements

CharityLens is designed to support responsible digital PR.

The system must not be designed to:

- Send mass unsolicited media emails
- Harvest private contact information
- Impersonate journalists or organizations
- Fabricate media interest
- Manufacture endorsements
- Generate false testimonials
- Create fabricated beneficiaries
- Invent donation outcomes
- Misrepresent fundraising urgency
- Manipulate donation statistics
- Circumvent platform restrictions
- Conceal material campaign information

Media organizations and journalists retain complete discretion over whether they respond, publish, link to, interview campaign organizers, or take no action.

## Transparency & Provenance

CharityLens should maintain provenance wherever practical.

The system should distinguish between:

- User-provided information
- Verified external information
- Retrieved public information
- Calculated results
- AI-generated recommendations
- AI-generated content
- Estimated values
- Predictions
- Confirmed campaign results

Where external information is used, the system should preserve the source, retrieval date, and relevant context whenever technically possible.

## Privacy

CharityLens should follow data minimization principles.

The system should:

- Collect only information necessary for campaign operations
- Avoid unnecessary personal data collection
- Avoid private contact harvesting
- Provide appropriate controls for stored campaign information
- Protect donor and beneficiary information
- Support deletion of unnecessary data
- Separate public campaign information from private operational information

## Project Goals

CharityLens aims to provide an open-source foundation for charitable campaign intelligence that helps users:

- Develop better campaign ideas
- Plan campaigns realistically
- Establish credibility
- Find appropriate funding infrastructure
- Reach relevant audiences
- Notify appropriate media organizations
- Improve donation experiences
- Track campaign progress
- Maintain transparency
- Understand campaign performance
- Demonstrate measurable impact

The goal is not to guarantee donations, publicity, media coverage, or campaign success. The goal is to give campaign organizers better tools, better information, and better structure for making informed decisions.
