# CivicTrust
*Verifying Actions, Protecting Communities*

---

# Project Overview

CivicTrust is an open-source, privacy-first accountability platform for autonomous agents. It provides a universal trust and governance layer that gives every agent a verifiable identity, human-anchored stewardship, traceable actions, and enforceable constitutional rules without relying on centralized identity providers, proprietary KYC systems, or blockchain-only solutions.

The platform is designed for AI agents, monitoring systems, autonomous services, security platforms, civic technologies, and distributed networks that require transparency, accountability, and privacy-preserving verification.
