# EternaSphere Specification

## Overview

**EternaSphere** is an open-source planetary intelligence platform that combines artificial intelligence, economic modeling, systems science, environmental analysis, and policy simulation into a unified framework. It continuously analyzes global data to simulate how decisions made today influence economic stability, environmental sustainability, public health, technological advancement, and human well-being over decades and centuries.

Rather than focusing on a single industry or government, EternaSphere is designed as a global simulation platform capable of modeling interactions between nations, corporations, institutions, ecosystems, and citizens. The platform provides transparent, explainable simulations that help researchers, governments, organizations, and communities evaluate long-term outcomes before real-world implementation.

Its modular architecture allows each domain to evolve independently while contributing to a unified planetary model.

# Long-Term Vision

EternaSphere is intended to become an open, transparent, continuously evolving planetary intelligence platform capable of helping humanity better understand the complex interactions between economics, technology, health, ecosystems, governance, and society. By combining modular AI systems with transparent simulations and collaborative research, the project aims to support more informed decision-making, encourage sustainable development, strengthen global resilience, and provide an open foundation for exploring long-term futures through evidence-based analysis.
