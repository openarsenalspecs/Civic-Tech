# Solvia AI – Fusion Unlocked

**Solvia AI** is the **first fully open-source AI platform for designing safe, zero-waste, disaster-resilient fusion reactors**. It empowers a global community of scientists, engineers, developers, educators, and students to collaborate on creating **clean, limitless, and disaster-proof fusion energy**.  

---

## 🌟 Mission
- Democratize fusion reactor design for **everyone**.  
- Optimize **safety, efficiency, and disaster resilience**.  
- Build a **global collaborative ecosystem** for zero-waste energy.  
- Educate the next generation in **fusion energy, AI-powered simulation, and disaster-resilient design**.  

---

## ⚡ Core Features

### **1. Advanced Simulation & Modeling**
- Multi-physics simulation: plasma, thermal, structural, neutron dynamics.  
- Real-time feedback for **safety, efficiency, and waste reduction**.  
- Disaster scenario simulations: earthquakes, hurricanes, floods, tsunamis.  
- Material testing simulator for **low-activation components**.  
- Long-term reactor lifecycle and wear simulation.  
- Quantum-level plasma modeling for next-gen research.  
- Environmental impact simulation: construction, cooling, energy distribution.  
- Hybrid AI + physical experiment simulations using sensor data.  

---

### **2. AI & Optimization**
- Generative AI for **novel reactor geometries**.  
- Multi-objective optimization: energy output, waste reduction, disaster resilience.  
- Predictive maintenance for reactor components.  
- Cross-reactor benchmarking with experimental reactors.  
- Explainable AI reports for human-readable design reasoning.  
- Adaptive AI that learns from simulations and community feedback.  
- Collaborative AI models working together for optimal design solutions.  
- Meta-learning AI improving over time from previous outcomes.  
- Explainable fusion pathways showing energy flow and waste reduction.

## 🚀 How to Use Solvia AI
1. Fork the repository: [https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/SolviaAI](https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/SolviaAI)  
2. Set up Python 3.10+ and install dependencies (`requirements.txt`).  
3. Input reactor parameters: energy output, plasma type, site location.  
4. Run AI simulations to generate optimized reactor designs.  
5. Review automated reports: **safety, disaster resilience, efficiency, waste reduction**.  
6. Collaborate with the community to refine designs, contribute AI models, simulations, visualizations, and tutorials.
