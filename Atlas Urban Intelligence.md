# Atlas Urban Intelligence

**Distributed intelligence for real-world motion.**

Atlas Urban Intelligence is a modular, AGPL 3.0+ smart city AI specification that transforms distributed edge sensor data into real-time, privacy-preserving urban intelligence. Built around an edge-first architecture and event-driven processing, Atlas enables cities, transportation agencies, campuses, industrial sites, and private infrastructure operators to monitor motion, improve operations, and respond to changing conditions without relying on persistent identity tracking or centralized surveillance.

The specification is designed to be modular from the ground up, allowing organizations to deploy only the components they require while extending the platform through optional plugin modules.

---

# Specification

Atlas Urban Intelligence defines an open, vendor-neutral architecture for building privacy-preserving smart city and intelligent infrastructure systems.

The specification establishes standards for:

- Distributed edge intelligence
- Event-driven urban data processing
- Privacy-first AI inference
- Modular system architecture
- Real-time operational awareness
- AI-assisted infrastructure management
- Open hardware compatibility
- Federated intelligence
- Explainable AI
- Civic transparency
- Open APIs and interoperability

Rather than storing continuous surveillance data, Atlas converts distributed sensor inputs into structured event intelligence that can be analyzed, shared, simulated, and acted upon while respecting privacy and local governance policies.

# Modular Design

Every component within Atlas Urban Intelligence is designed as an independent module.

Modules may be:

- Added
- Removed
- Replaced
- Upgraded
- Extended

without requiring changes to the remainder of the system.

Deployments may consist of only the required modules while expanding over time through optional plugins.
