# OpenCareerForecast

### Discover the True Cost of Your Career Path.

OpenCareerForecast is an AGPL 3.0+ open-source career intelligence platform that helps students, families, career changers, counselors, workforce agencies, employers, and policymakers evaluate the financial viability of education and career decisions.

The platform compares colleges, universities, trade schools, apprenticeships, certifications, military careers, and entrepreneurial pathways using real-world education costs, wage data, cost-of-living information, debt analysis, and future labor market forecasting.

OpenCareerForecast helps answer a critical question:

> Will this career path improve my financial future?

## Core Objectives

- Provide transparent education ROI analysis.
- Compare degree costs against career earnings.
- Evaluate affordability based on state-specific living expenses.
- Forecast future career demand and income trends.
- Identify financially sustainable career pathways.
- Recommend alternative educational and career options.
- Deliver open, auditable calculations and forecasting models.
