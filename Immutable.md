# Immutable

**Life tracked, truth protected.**

Immutable is an open-source, encrypted life tracking database designed to preserve a complete, verifiable, and citizen-controlled record of identity, legal history, residency, and official documentation across state and federal systems. It addresses long-standing inconsistencies in public records, including historical documentation gaps, clerical variations, and fragmented agency systems.

---

## Overview

Immutable provides a unified, encrypted ledger of life events and official records. It is designed for:

- Citizens managing their verified identity history
- Researchers and genealogists working with historical records
- Agencies requiring consistent identity and residency verification
- Systems needing privacy-preserving, auditable data integrity

All records are encrypted, permission-controlled, and append-only to preserve integrity and traceability over time.
