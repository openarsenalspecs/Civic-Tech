# ProductionMind

**Connecting Every Machine, Process, and Decision.**

ProductionMind is an open-source, graph-native manufacturing intelligence platform designed to create a connected operational model of modern industry. Built with a modular architecture, ProductionMind transforms factories into intelligent ecosystems by connecting machines, processes, materials, people, data, and decisions through a unified graph-based system.

ProductionMind enables predictive maintenance, digital twins, industrial AI, supply chain intelligence, quality optimization, and autonomous manufacturing workflows while providing an open foundation for the next generation of smart factories.

## Vision

Manufacturing systems today are often fragmented across separate platforms for production, maintenance, quality, inventory, supply chains, and analytics. ProductionMind creates a unified intelligence layer where every component of manufacturing becomes connected.

By representing industrial operations as an evolving graph, ProductionMind enables factories to understand relationships, detect patterns, predict failures, optimize workflows, and continuously improve.
