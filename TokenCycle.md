# TokenCycle 2.0
*Circular Infrastructure, Rewarded*

TokenCycle 2.0 is an **open-source platform** for building **circular infrastructure systems** in cities. The platform integrates **waste-to-energy, recycling, composting, AI, IoT, blockchain, and community token economies** to transform how cities manage materials, energy, and citizen engagement.

---

## Overview

TokenCycle allows cities and communities to:

- Track waste streams and materials in real time  
- Optimize recycling and composting operations  
- Recover energy from waste and integrate with microgrids  
- Reward citizens through a token-based system for sustainable behavior  
- Simulate city-wide impacts using digital twins and AI predictive models  
- Ensure resilient operations during emergencies with redundant microgrids and rapid-deployment modules  

---

## Features

The platform is designed with **modular, scalable, and technology-driven features**:

### 1. Waste & Material Management
- Smart bins with fill-level monitoring  
- AI contamination detection  
- Robotic sorting integration  
- Composting hubs with process monitoring  
- Recycling plant interfaces  
- Industrial symbiosis support  
- Landfill diversion analytics  

### 2. Energy & Microgrid
- Waste-to-Energy units  
- Biogas digesters  
- Battery storage integration  
- Hybrid renewable energy microgrid  
- AI predictive load balancing  
- Carbon-negative energy processes  
- Energy tokenization for community trading  

### 3. AI & Analytics
- Edge AI for on-device sorting  
- Predictive waste generation models  
- Adaptive collection routing  
- Material lifecycle tracking  
- Digital twin of city-wide flows  
- Sustainability scoring dashboards  
- Policy simulation & optimization recommendations  

### 4. Blockchain & Token Economy
- Citizen token rewards for recycling and green behavior  
- Smart contracts for material trading and marketplace operations  
- NFT-based impact badges for gamification  
- Carbon credit integration with token economy  
- Transparent blockchain ledger for material flows  

### 5. IoT & Sensor Network
- Smart bin sensors (LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, 5G)  
- Environmental sensors (temperature, moisture, gas)  
- Fleet telematics for collection vehicles  
- Edge AI on IoT devices  
- Self-calibrating sensors  
- Interconnected city sensors for multi-data integration  

### 6. Community Engagement
- AR citizen guides for waste & energy flows  
- Gamified sustainability challenges with token rewards  
- Public dashboards for material, energy, and token tracking  
- Community science and data contribution programs  

### 7. Resilience & Disaster Adaptation
- Rapid deployment modules for emergencies  
- Disaster simulation mode (floods, hurricanes, outages)  
- Redundant microgrids to ensure critical services  
- Community alert systems for collection or resource updates  

### 8. Developer & Contributor Features
- Modular microservice architecture for backend  
- Cloud and hybrid environment deployment  
- Edge AI model management and over-the-air updates  
- CI/CD pipelines for automated testing and deployment  
- Semantic versioning with CHANGELOG.md tracking  
- Contributor guidelines and documentation in `/docs`  

### 9. Optional Advanced Features
- Autonomous robotic collection and delivery  
- Smart chemical/material recovery  
- Industrial IoT integration with factories  
- Carbon market participation via tokens  
- Predictive maintenance of collection and energy equipment  
- AI-driven citizen behavior recommendations
